# Academic work and delivery

## Establish the assignment contract

Before drafting assessed work, locate the actual assignment, rubric and applicable
course/program guidance. Capture the question, audience/language, required
output, scope/length, assessment criteria, method, deadline, citation style and
AI-use/disclosure rules in the existing assignment note. Record source, version,
retrieval date and applicability. Use the optional
[assignment template](../../00-system/templates/agent/assignment.md) only when
there is no suitable note. Keep unknowns explicit; do not force every field for
a small task. Continue independent preparation while a consequential gap is open.

Use current applicable institutional rules plus valid course-specific requirements.
If they conflict, retain the conflict and establish authority rather than choosing
the more convenient rule. The older local scientific-writing guide is background,
not proof of current typography, permitted source types or AI rules. For the
checked 2026 institutional baseline see [reference-basis.md](reference-basis.md).
Recheck the official source when relying on it for a new assessed task.

## From question to defensible text

Narrow the question enough that the available time, evidence and method can
answer it. Outline the argument and map rubric criteria to intended sections.
Link consequential claims to evidence through [research.md](research.md).
Distinguish definitions, empirical findings, interpretation and recommendation.
Use the discipline's appropriate structure; do not force IMRaD on every essay.

Draft in the requested language and preserve the user's argument and voice.
Each substantive paragraph should advance a claim, supply appropriate evidence
and explain its relation to the question. Define terms consistently. Surface an
unsupported claim as an open gap; do not hide it behind polished wording. Give
specific feedback tied to a passage and criterion, not generic praise or a made-up
grade. Keep the user able to explain and defend the resulting reasoning.

Match every in-text citation with its bibliography entry and vice versa where
the required style demands it. Verify quotes against the original and use the
required locator. Clearly identify secondary citations, translations, adapted
figures and reused own text. Keep citation fields editable in the author's format.
Do not claim a similarity score, plagiarism clearance or AI-detector result without
an actual relevant check, and do not treat such a result as proof of integrity.

## Empirical work and calculations

Before collecting data, establish the research question, sampling/recruitment,
consent and applicable institutional ethics requirements. Distinguish a teaching
exercise from research with participants. Use approved consent and storage
arrangements; do not upload identifiable interviews or sensitive datasets to a
new service without specific authority. Synthetic examples must be labeled.

Preserve raw data. Keep derived data separate, with a data dictionary, units,
missing-value treatment, exclusions and transformation steps. Record actual
software/package versions and random seeds when they affect reproducibility.
For quantitative work justify the analysis against the question, check assumptions
and report uncertainty; avoid selective outcomes and causal overstatement.
For qualitative work link claims to pseudonymized excerpts and locators, preserve
the coding decisions and consider contradictory cases. Never fabricate responses,
sample size, saturation, measurements or significance. Check calculations and
recreate final tables/figures from the documented inputs when feasible.

## AI transparency and collaboration

Apply the verified rule for the specific work; neither assume all AI assistance
is forbidden nor assume blanket permission for an assessment. If the applicable
permission remains unknown, continue allowed preparation and identify the gap
before claiming the work complies. Follow any known restriction.

Update an existing AI log for assessed work, or use the optional
[AI-use-log template](../../00-system/templates/agent/ai-use-log.md)
when disclosure is relevant. Record actual date, tool/model if known, purpose,
material input summary, output used/rejected and verification. Unknown model
versions remain unknown. Separate agent checks, user-reported review and direct
human review evidence; never sign an authenticity declaration for the user.
Keep confidential material out of prompts and logs.

For group work, record agreed owners, contributions, dependencies and unresolved
decisions in the existing assignment note. Attribute actual contributions;
do not invent consent or send messages to team members without authorization.

## Delivery check

Check content against every applicable rubric item, source/citation consistency,
unresolved placeholders, required disclosures, appendices, filename and format.
Use the relevant document/PDF/slides skill for export and inspect the actual
export for clipping, missing glyphs, broken references and unreadable graphics.
For a presentation, check the requested duration and whether the speaker can
explain the argument; export success proves neither rehearsal nor comprehension.

Record `draft`, `ready_for_user_review`, `ready_to_submit` or `submitted` with
evidence. Blocking requirement gaps prevent `ready_to_submit`. `submitted`
requires an authorized submission and a receipt/status confirming the correct
artifact and destination. Preparing a ZIP or PDF is not submission. Report failed
or unavailable checks precisely while completing the rest of the authorized work.
